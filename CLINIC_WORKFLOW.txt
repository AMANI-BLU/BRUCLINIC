BRUCLINIC CLINICAL WORKFLOW

1. Patient check-in
Reception finds an existing patient or registers a new patient. Blood group may be left unrecorded. Reception records the reason for the visit, urgency, and destination, then sends the patient to triage or the doctor.

2. Triage
Nursing staff record the required observations and forward the patient to the doctor. Allergies and existing patient history should be reviewed before treatment decisions.

3. Doctor review and laboratory requests
The doctor opens the active visit and starts at Lab Request. A final diagnosis is not required to request an investigation. Select or enter one test, its category, specimen, urgency, and clinical indication. For a medical certificate, record the certificate purpose and request investigations appropriate to the assessment; BruClinic does not apply a fixed certificate panel.

Each laboratory investigation is a separate order. The laboratory records specimen collection, analysis, result, and verification. A completed result returns the visit to the doctor queue for review. The doctor may request another test if clinically needed.

4. Assessment
The doctor records the diagnosis supported by the history, examination, and available results, along with symptoms and clinical notes. Existing diagnoses with similar spelling may be suggested; the doctor must verify and select a suggestion. Suggestions never change a diagnosis automatically.

5. Prescription, if needed
The doctor selects a medicine from the formulary when available and records dose, frequency, duration, quantity, and patient instructions. For each catalog item, choose Clinic pharmacy only when enough stock is available; otherwise choose Outside purchase or select another medicine. A custom non-catalogue medicine is routed as an outside purchase. Pharmacy reviews the selected source and may change it if stock changes before dispensing. To replenish a clinic item, use the separate Pharmacy inventory restock action.

6. Complete the visit
The doctor completes the visit when assessment and any required actions are finished. A visit with no lab or prescription can be completed directly after assessment. Completed visits are read-only; a new consultation starts with a new reception visit.

REPORTING NOTES
Diagnosis capitalization and extra spaces are normalized for case-category reporting. Similar spellings are not automatically merged because they may represent different clinical conditions. Choose a verified existing diagnosis suggestion when appropriate.
